Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) - 2nd Year

The second year of our Bachelor of Commerce program transitions students into complex institutional accounting paradigms and analytical fiscal regulations. The curriculum focuses heavily on building core competence in corporate reporting systems, direct taxation laws, cost metrics compilation, and electronic commerce frameworks to prepare students for corporate financial analyst and management positions.

Duration: 1 Year (2 Semesters — Sem III & Sem IV)
Eligibility: Successful clearance and promotion from B.Com 1st Year according to academic thresholds.

2nd Year Core Subjects

CAC

Corporate Accounting

Master corporate metrics regarding share issues, company liquidation steps, debenture values, and consolidated corporate balance sheets.

ITL

Income Tax Law

Study structural direct tax calculations across personal salaries, house property assets, business profits, and capital gains.

CAT

Cost Accounting

Analyze material cost allocations, overhead computation parameters, process ledger accounts, and break-even output points.

CPL

Company Law

Understand corporate formation steps, legal charters, memorandum frameworks, share capital provisions, and boardroom administration guidelines.

ECM

E-Commerce Business

Explore digital marketplace infrastructures, secure electronic transactional payment conduits, system data laws, and web safety metrics.

CAB

Business Application IT

Leverage enterprise spreadsheet formulas, corporate database architecture patterns, data queries, and automated financial tracking setups.

BCM

Business Communication

Develop trade dialogue criteria, technical report formats, interactive interview processes, and corporate communication metrics.

GEL

General Elective Paper

Select specialized minor tracks including Consumer Protection frameworks, Macroeconomic indices, or alternative regional modern languages.

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) - 3rd Year

The final year of the B.Com program shifts completely toward corporate governance strategy, audit processes, and high-level applied financial disciplines. Students explore global trade networks alongside indirect taxation metrics while engaging in an integrated corporate project framework, ensuring they exit the college as highly competent, employment-ready commerce leaders.

Duration: 1 Year (2 Semesters — Sem V & Sem VI)
Eligibility: Academic clearance of the minimum required credits up to B.Com 2nd Year (Sem IV).

Enroll in B.Com

3rd Year Advanced Subjects

ACG

Auditing & Governance

Implement formal internal auditing vouchers, verified balance checks, company ethics frameworks, and regulatory control practices.

ITG

Indirect Taxes & GST

Understand dynamic Good and Services Tax mechanics, dual GST processing configurations, custom tariffs, and supply place parameters.

FMG

Financial Management

Evaluate corporate capital formatting costs, working capital management cycles, asset deployment models, and dividend policies.

MAC

Management Accounting

Utilize financial statement analysis techniques, asset ratio processing equations, fund statement flows, and variance planning data.

INB

International Business

Analyze globalization trends, foreign entry choices, currency exchange vectors, and international trade policy mandates.

FOI

Investment Basics

Study capital markets operations, equity evaluation equations, mutual fund portfolios, and bond returns metrics.

EDP

Entrepreneurship

Formulate business model sheets, funding acquisition steps, risk parameters, and corporate venture execution frameworks.

CTP

Corporate Tax Planning

Learn structural tax avoidance vs evasion guidelines, deduction choices, and legal strategies to optimize company tax burdens.
