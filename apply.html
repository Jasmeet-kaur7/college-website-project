Online Admission Portal

Welcome to the Horizon College Online Admission Portal. Please read the instructions carefully before starting your application.

Admission Instructions

Applicants are required to register before filling the admission form. Please make sure that the information provided during registration is correct.

New Applicant

  • Students applying for admission for the first time should register as a new applicant.
  • Use a valid mobile number and email address during registration.
  • Keep your academic information ready before starting the application.

Already Registered Applicant

  • Students who have already registered can sign in using their registered email and password.
  • Do not create another account if you have already registered.

Important Information

  • Fill all information carefully.
  • Select the correct course.
  • Check your information before submitting.
  • Keep your application details safe.

If you have already registered, sign in to continue your admission application.
