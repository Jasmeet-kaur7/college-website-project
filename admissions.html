Join Horizon College

Horizon College welcomes students who are passionate about learning and building a bright future. Our admission process is designed to be simple, transparent, and student-friendly.

Students can choose from a range of programmes in Arts, Science, and Commerce according to their interests and career goals.

Eligibility Criteria

🎓

Undergraduate

Students who have successfully completed Class 12 or an equivalent examination can apply for undergraduate programmes.

📚

Academic Record

Admission is based on academic qualifications and the eligibility requirements of the selected programme.

📝

Application

Applicants must complete the admission form and provide the required information and supporting documents.
