Bachelor of Arts (BA)

Our Bachelor of Arts program offers a vibrant, interdisciplinary environment designed to foster critical thinking, creativity, and analytical skills. Students explore human culture, society, and behavior through diverse subject areas.

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters)
Eligibility: 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board.

Enroll in BA

Available Specializations

ENG

English Literature

Explore classic and contemporary global texts while refining your advanced writing and critical analysis skills.

PSY

Psychology

Understand human behavior, cognition, mental health, and the scientific research methods behind social science.

POL

Political Science

Analyze political systems, global governance, international relations, and public policies shaping our world.

HIS

History & Culture

Examine historical events, civilizations, and historical shifts that have formed the modern global landscape.

SOC

Sociology

Study social behaviors, cultural patterns, and the structures of institutions that shape human communities.

ECO

Economics

Analyze how societies allocate resources, wealth distribution, market trends, and global economic systems.

JMC

Journalism & Media

Learn print journalism, digital broadcasting, media ethics, and modern communication strategies.

PHI

Philosophy

Delve into fundamental questions about existence, logic, human ethics, knowledge, and critical reasoning.
