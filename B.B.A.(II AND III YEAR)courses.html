Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) - 2nd Year

The second year of our Bachelor of Business Administration program shifts students from basic managerial theory into tactical corporate execution and core functional analytics. The curriculum focuses heavily on building core competence in consumer marketing behaviors, analytical human resource allocation, data-driven marketing frameworks, and specialized corporate financial modeling to prepare students for real-world managerial operations.

Duration: 1 Year (2 Semesters — Sem III & Sem IV)
Eligibility: Successful academic clearance and promotion from BBA 1st Year according to university board standards.

2nd Year Tactical Modules

MKM

Marketing Management

Master consumer segmentation rules, targeting practices, positioning grids, the standard marketing mix, and brand equity metrics.

HRM

Human Resource Management

Analyze talent acquisition pipelines, performance appraisal matrices, corporate compensation modeling, and strategic employee retention methods.

FMG

Financial Management

Evaluate corporate capital formatting costs, working capital management cycles, long-term asset deployment, and dividend distribution setups.

POM

Operations Management

Optimize plant location logistics, structural inventory control loops, supply chain configurations, and rigorous quality assurance protocols.

BRM

Research Methodology

Design empirical research frameworks, curate field sampling metrics, compile survey matrices, and process structural data variables via software.

CLF

Corporate Law

Study regulatory statutes governing the Companies Act, industrial dispute resolution, consumer safety laws, and modern factory parameters.

CNB

Consumer Behaviour

Investigate psychological buyer motivations, cognitive purchase decision trees, environmental triggers, and tracking analytics.

MEC

Managerial Economics

Apply microeconomic theory optimization metrics to real-world corporate forecasting, risk assessment vectors, and pricing strategy.

Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) - 3rd Year

The final year of the BBA program transitions completely toward high-level corporate governance strategy, enterprise entrepreneurship, and global market integration. Students choose deep specialization pathways while engaging in an intensive management project framework, ensuring they graduate as agile, deployment-ready corporate leaders capable of piloting modern businesses.

Duration: 1 Year (2 Semesters — Sem V & Sem VI)
Eligibility: Academic clearance of the minimum required credits up to BBA 2nd Year (Sem IV).

Enroll in BBA

3rd Year Strategic & Specialization Subjects

SMG

Strategic Management

Formulate corporate SWOT matrices, evaluate competitive industry frameworks, map mergers, and control long-term growth policy setups.

EDP

Entrepreneurship

Construct comprehensive business model sheets, outline initial capital incubation pipelines, pitch models, and minimize market entry risks.

IBM

International Business

Analyze globalization trends, foreign direct investment entry choices, global trade pacts, and cross-border currency vectors.

BEG

Ethics & Governance

Implement strict boardroom accountability criteria, sustainable environmental social metrics (ESG), and moral corporate practices.

FMS

Financial Markets

Explore capital market equity mechanics, banking merchant options, underwriting provisions, and credit rating system setups.

DMM

Digital Marketing

Optimize search engine visibility parameters, analyze paid conversion loops, deploy social ad sets, and run content campaigns.

ERM

Retail & E-Commerce

Learn structural inventory supply loops, physical layout logistics, digital storefront models, and secure transaction systems.

PRJ

Capstone Project Work

Succeed in multi-week industrial market testing, compile a structural corporate thesis file, and pass the final evaluation panel reviews.
