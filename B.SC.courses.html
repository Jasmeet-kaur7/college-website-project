Our Bachelor of Science(B.Sc.)

Our Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) program offers a rigorous, laboratory-driven academic environment designed to foster analytical reasoning, experimental skills, and scientific inquiry. Students explore the fundamental principles of the physical and natural world through diverse subject streams. The curriculum perfectly balances comprehensive theoretical lectures with structured practical lab sessions, preparing graduates for high-tier research, industrial operations, and competitive corporate tech roles.

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters).
Eligibility:10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology / Biotechnology and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, securing a minimum of 50% aggregate marks.

Enroll in B.SC.

Available Specializations

ENG

Technical English

Build scientific research drafting, technical documentation writing, and professional seminar presentation skills.

PHY

Core Physics

Study classical mechanics, quantum wave optics, electromagnetic fields, and thermodynamic principles.

CHM

Chemical Sciences

Explore atomic structures, dynamic organic reaction steps, thermodynamics, and molecular bonding equations.

MTH

Advanced Mathematics

Master differential calculus, complex vector spaces, linear matrices, and operational numerical modeling.

BOT

Plant Biology (Botany)

Analyze plant physiology, cellular anatomy, diverse ecological lifecycles, and genetic modification.

ZOO

Animal Sciences (Zoology)

Examine structural vertebrate anatomy, evolutionary biology, metabolic processes, and animal behavior profiles.

EVS

Environmental Studies

Evaluate global biodiversity conservation, ecosystem sustainability metrics, and environmental audit paths.
